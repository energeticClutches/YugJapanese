# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** July 2026

YugJapanese ("the app") is an offline-first Japanese learning application
for Android. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use
it.

## The short version

The app does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data, because it
has no way to. There are no servers, no accounts, and no network requests
of any kind.

## What data is stored, and where

All of your learning progress, review schedules, quiz results, analytics,
achievements, and settings are stored **only on your device**, in a local
Hive database. This data:

- Never leaves your device.
- Is not sent to the developer, to Google, or to any third party.
- Is not backed up to any cloud service by the app itself (though your
  device's own OS-level backup, if enabled, may include it — that is
  controlled by your Android settings, not by this app).

## Network access

The app makes no network requests. It does not use the internet
permission for analytics, ads, crash reporting, or content delivery.
Every lesson, audio clip, and image is bundled inside the app itself.

## Third-party services

The app does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or
tracking SDKs.

## Data deletion

You can erase all locally stored data at any time via
**Settings → Reset progress**, or by uninstalling the app, which removes
its local database entirely.

## Children's privacy

Because the app collects no personal data of any kind, it is safe for use
by learners of any age.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes (for example, if a future version adds
optional cloud sync), the update will be reflected here and in the app's
changelog before that feature ships.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the developer via the
project's GitHub repository.
